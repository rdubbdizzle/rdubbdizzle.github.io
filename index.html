Three moves that get you on

01

Sign up early

Email the terminal with leave papers as soon as your window opens. Seniority inside your category is the date-time of signup, not when you mark present.

02

Watch the 72-hour

Boards only firm up about three days out. Quiet weeks are normal. The recording and PDF beat rumors every time.

03

Be present at roll call

Mark present, then stand there when they call categories. A “0T” or TBD can still turn into a jet. Missing roll call is how people miss the jet.
