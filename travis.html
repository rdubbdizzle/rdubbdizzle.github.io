How to sign up from Travis

Email the 60 APS Space-A desk. Do not send SSN or date of birth. Include rank, full name, branch, status, dependents traveling, seats needed, eligibility category, leave dates, and up to five destinations. Patriot Express rotations to Hickam fill fast — show early for roll call.

Travis posts a new dated 72-hour file most days (TRAVIS_72HRS_DDMMMYY.pdf). The 24-hour roll-call PDF is yesterday’s results, not tomorrow’s seats.
